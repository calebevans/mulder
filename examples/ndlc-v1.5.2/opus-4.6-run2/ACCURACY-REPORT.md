## Analysis of Misses and Errors

### The source-name collision, mirrored (item 4; FP 2)

The #228 fix again did its job: `detect_masquerading` returned 17 lines on rm2, 0 on rm1, and the report cites "The tsk.masquerade detection identified 17 deleted files on the rm2 FAT32 partition". The extractor-side outputs are per-image and right. In the first v1.5.2 run the rm1 agent was the victim (it received rm2's masquerade rows and gave RM1 a FAT32 partition); in this run the rm2 agent was (it received rm1's `tsk.timeline` and `tsk.fsstat` rows and gave RM2 an exFAT partition, RM1's volume serial and RM1's Feb 15 copy).
